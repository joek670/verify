Trust needs more than an AI detector.

Inspect image and voice files, run a live camera and microphone challenge, then apply an explainable allow, review, or block policy.

Important limit

No detector can prove that media is human-made. Missing metadata is not proof, and AI metadata can be removed. Use this as a risk gate, not an accusation.

Check an image or voice file

Reads the file locally for format mismatches, specific generation markers, and unvalidated Content Credentials-like text.

JPEG, PNG, WAV, MP3, or OGG. Maximum 50 MB.

Check camera and microphone activity

Speaks a generated two-turn challenge and recognizes the reply on this device, then checks for sustained audio and frame-to-frame visual activity. It does not verify the requested movement and cannot stop a relayed response.

          Why this never reaches Allow.
          The spoken words are recognized on the same device being questioned, and the requested movement is never checked, so the live result keeps a floor of 35 however well the challenge goes. Lifting that floor would take a server-issued challenge bound to a signed nonce and scored outside this browser. Until then the best honest outcome is Review.
